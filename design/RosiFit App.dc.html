Where to tap
Sign in with any 4 digits — or CE to clear, Forgot PIN for the security questions, and Super admin registration for the recovery answers. Seven tabs: Home (Academy wise / Branch wise sub-tabs, chart first, then the cards that lead to Upload and Compose), Members, Courses, Weekly (a row opens Member detail), Sessions (month view — tap a day), Reports (pie per member, course or branch) and More (profile, appearance with six accents plus any custom hue, follow-up rules, templates, holidays, staff and access). Upload runs the full CSV → five-outcome review → import path.
