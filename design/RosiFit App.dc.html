Where to tap
Overview and Attendance are the two tabs under the academy name; Home · Reports · More sit in the footer. Branch is a filter, not a header control. Overview holds the Academy wise / Branch wise view, the Branch / Course / Period filters, and the attendance chart — and nothing after it. Branches can be added under More → Configuration, and Appearance has preset colours, a custom hue or hex, and a live app preview. Attendance is the workspace: Add Course opens as a dialog (name, branch, frequency days — at least one required, from-email, template, and the 4-weekly / 4-consecutive follow-up rule), then tap a course for its detail — Branch and Date filter dropdowns with a tappable day strip (This week is the default), Send Communication, Members with Add Member and Bulk Import, Upload Attendance and Weekly Review. Editing a course is done from its card on the list. A course's message wording, sender and follow-up rule are edited in the course form itself — there is no separate Message Templates or Follow-up Rules screen in settings. Reports has KPIs, attended-vs-missed bars per course, branch or member, a week-by-week trend and Export; the audit log records in-app actions — member added, attendance uploaded, communication sent, course edited, match decided — attributed to whoever performed them. The header bell shows upload and communication activity. Sign in by number, then any 4 digits. Registered numbers: 80563 29742 (Shazia, super admin), 98431 55210 (Aliya, staff), 90032 71144 (Sowmya, staff). An unknown number goes to super admin registration — details, two security questions, then you set your own PIN and land signed in. Issued PINs are only for staff, from Staff & access. A number with no account goes to registration; staff sign in as themselves and have no Staff & access under More. CE clears the PIN, Forgot PIN runs the security questions.
