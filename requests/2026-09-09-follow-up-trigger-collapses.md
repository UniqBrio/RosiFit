## OPEN QUESTIONS — readings taken, not settled by the requester

- **Q1. What does the collapsed card still say?** Not stated, and this is the load-bearing one.
  Reading: **the heading row plus the trigger IN FORCE, in one generated line.** The panel exists
  because *"saying nothing at all is what this change exists to stop"* (its own round-1 note), and
  a card shrunk to *Follow-up trigger* over a list of 435 people states that a rule exists without
  saying what it is. The line is `triggerSummary` in `src/data/followupTrigger.ts`, so C-67 holds
  and a spec can pin it.
- **Q2. Which number does the collapsed line state — the saved one or the stepper's?** Not stated.
  Reading: **the saved one**, always. The list below the card was drawn from the saved rule, and a
  collapsed card quoting an unapplied draft would have the app claiming a rule it is not acting on.
- **Q3. Can it be collapsed with a change typed and not applied?** Not stated. Reading: **yes, but
  it is NAMED** — the collapsed line carries `… · N missed sessions in a week typed, not applied`.
  Refusing the press would be a control that silently does nothing; hiding the disagreement would
  leave the stepper and the rule apart with nothing on screen to say so. The pending half is
  phrased weekly because that is what Apply would write — the same conversion `triggerConverts`
  already warns about.
- **Q4. Does a failed save survive the collapse?** Not stated. Reading: **yes.** The failure line
  is drawn outside both branches. Shrinking the card is a tidying press; it must not be a way to
  make *"Nothing has been changed"* disappear off a screen whose next control sends email.
- **Q5. Is the collapsed state remembered?** Not stated. Reading: **no** — part 3 says *"all
  time"*, so this is plain component state with no storage of any kind. Every mount, on every
  screen, on every reopen of the dialog, starts expanded.
- **Q6. Does Apply collapse it?** Reading: **no.** *"They can manually click on it"* is a control
  offered after the work, not a card that folds itself the moment a save lands. A panel that moved
  on its own while somebody was reading the number it holds would be the opposite of the point.
- **Q7. Does the chevron carry a word?** Not stated. Reading: **yes** — `expand_more`/`expand_less`
  with *Show*/*Hide* beside it, which is the idiom `TokenChips` and the audit groups already use,
  and **guardrail 3** is why the glyph does not carry the state alone. `accessibilityState`
  announces `expanded` as well.
- **Q8. Does it apply to the Reach out pop-up too?** The screenshot is the send dialog, but the
  card is one shared component and splitting it would be two panels to drift apart — the very
  thing its round-1 note refuses. Reading: **both screens get it.**
